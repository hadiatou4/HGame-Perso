Titre du projet : Space-war Hedera-Based Multiplayer NFT Game

An immersive space combat game integrating an NFT reward system on the Hedera blockchain.


contexte 
Players don’t truly own their game items.

If the server shuts down → achievements disappear.

Everything is centralized and easy to lose.

What if every achievement you earn in a game truly belonged to you — forever?


SLIDE 3 — Introducing Space-War On-Chain Engine (Powered by Hedera)
A model of digital ownership built on Hedera Hashgraph

Game achievements become portable, verifiable assets stored on-chain.

Players earn  unique NFT rewards (“War Badges”) for skills, missions, and milestones.


NFTs cannot be faked or duplicated → minting requires verifiable gameplay.

Assets gain authenticity because they come from real player performance, not the server.

A6: Business Model

Revenue Streams:
 - Marketplace fees: 2% per NFT trade
 - Premium achievements: Rare NFT drops
 - In-game cosmetics: Ship skins, effects
 - Tournament entry fees: Competitive modes

Who Pays:
 - Players (NFT purchases & trades)
 - Tournaments (entry fees split)
 - Sponsors (tournament prizes)

Pricing Example:
 - NFT trades: 50-200 HBAR (2% fee = 1-4 HBAR)
 - Tournament entry: 10 HBAR per player
 - Premium skins: 25-100 HBAR

Scale Projection:
 - 1K active players = ~$500/month
 - 10K players = ~$5K/month
 - 100K players = ~$50K/month

Financial Flow:
Player → NFT Trade → 2% to Protocol
Player → Tournament → Prize pool distribution
Sponsor → Tournament → Community growth
